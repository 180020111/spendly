Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Spendly service, we collect the information you give us directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense entries you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type and access times, to help keep the Service secure and reliable.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate your account, display your expense summaries and spending breakdowns, and improve the features of the Service. Your data is used only to provide you with the functionality of Spendly and is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your data at any time by deleting your account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting, email delivery, or analytics. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose. We do not share your personal expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page or the Spendly support channel.
